Our Story

It all began in a small kitchen in Chennai, where our founder Rajan Pillai dreamed of bringing the bold, vibrant flavours of South India to a wider audience. In 2005, he opened Spice Junction with just five tables, a handful of family recipes, and an unwavering passion for authentic cooking. From the very first day, the aroma of freshly ground spices and slow-cooked curries drew in curious neighbours and loyal regulars alike.

Over two decades, Spice Junction grew from that tiny neighbourhood spot into a beloved dining destination. We stayed true to our roots — no shortcuts, no artificial flavours, no compromise on quality. Every masala is still freshly ground in-house each morning. Every biryani is still cooked on dum the traditional way. Our chefs train for months before they are allowed to serve a single dish to our guests.

Today, we proudly serve over 400 guests a day and have been featured in several food magazines and travel guides. But what matters most to us is still the same thing it was on day one — that you leave our table feeling deeply satisfied, as if you just had a home-cooked meal at a grandmother's house. Welcome to Spice Junction. This is your table.
